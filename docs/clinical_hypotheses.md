# Biological hypotheses and what is actually measured

This prototype investigates associations and individual deviations. The source
does not measure cortisol, sympathetic nerve activity, endothelial function or
incident events. No arrow in a proposed pathway is identified causally here.

1. Psychological strain may accompany altered autonomic regulation. A proposed
   HPA/sympathetic pathway motivates studying mental-health burden alongside HRV,
   heart-rate and sleep variables. The mechanism is a hypothesis, not a measured
   mediator model, and the source stress-score direction is unknown.
2. Disrupted sleep may affect physiological recovery. NHLBI describes changes
   in heart rate and blood pressure across sleep stages and links inadequate sleep
   with chronic health risks. A single sleep summary cannot establish that pathway.
3. Metabolic variables and historical conditions may mark cardiovascular phenotype.
   Their temporal order relative to diagnosis is unknown; prospective prediction
   and causal claims are therefore inappropriate.
4. Respiratory deviations are a reason to investigate a physiological pattern.
   Synthetic nocturnal desaturation is not a sleep-apnea diagnosis or cardiac-event label.

The original dataset's device units, aggregation periods, patient linkage and
measurement scales were not verified. Simulation units are explicit engineering
assumptions and are not a retrospective interpretation of the original fields.
